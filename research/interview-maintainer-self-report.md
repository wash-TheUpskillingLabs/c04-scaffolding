# Interview: a maintainer's own experience as a past participant (self-report)

Filed as issue #26.

1. What it is
A pod maintainer's own account, as a past participant, of two things: the
Learning Log, and sprints.

2. Where it came from
A pod maintainer who took part in Civics & Elections (Build Cycle 3) and
attended the Oct 3 retreat, writing about their own experience.

3. When
Experience Jul to Oct 2026. Written Nov 10, 2026.

4. What it says

The Learning Log
- The maintainer's own dashboard showed 8 weeks without a log (viewed Nov
  5, 2026).
- At the Oct 3 retreat, many participants reported not filling out the
  Learning Log, and said it wasn't as helpful as it could be (heard
  firsthand by the maintainer).

Sprints
- The maintainer didn't realize the Cycle was organized in sprints until
  after it ended.
- It would have helped to outline each sprint's goals, the type of work to
  expect, the deliverables due and by when, and tools and methods one might
  use. The participant interviewed Nov 9 (#23) said something similar, in
  terms of weeks rather than sprints: "these are the tools you can use, and
  these are the expectations... this week is going to be for this purpose...
  more deadlines."

[mine]

6. Is any of this hearsay?
Self-report. The retreat reports are what other participants said, heard
firsthand; there's no written record or count. The retreat survey or an
organizer could confirm.
