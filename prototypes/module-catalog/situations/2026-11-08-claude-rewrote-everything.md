Made-up example for the prototype. Not a real situation.

## The problem

Two participants asked Claude to "fix the page" and it rewrote far more than they wanted. They didn't know how to get back to what they had.

## What worked

Asking for one small change at a time, and reading the diff before accepting it. We also found their last commit, which got them back.
