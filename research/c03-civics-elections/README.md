# Record: How Civics & Elections (Build Cycle 3) actually ran

Filed as issue #24.

1. What it is
A participant's account of how Build Cycle 3 (Civics & Elections) ran from
sensemaking to pods, set beside the Luma event pages and the dashboard's
12-week Build Cycle timeline.

2. Where it came from
A pod maintainer who took part in Cycle 3; the 15 emails participants
received during the Cycle and the guides they linked to (attached below);
Luma event pages for The Upskilling Labs; the Upskilling App dashboard.
Screenshots taken by the maintainer, Nov 5, 2026.

3. When
Cycle 3 ran Jul 25 to Oct 13, 2026 (Luma). Participant emails Jul 27 to Oct
17, 2026. Account written Nov 9, 2026.

4. What it says

How it ran (the participant's account)
- An initial round of voting in weeks 1 and 2 was part of the Sensemaking
  Sprint.
- Luma said pods would form at the Sensemaking Sprint (Jul 25). They
  didn't.
- Problem statement proposals were presented at Meet the Pods (Aug 11).
- Three pod proposals were voted on (exact timing not recalled). Only one,
  "Effort vs Impact," got enough votes, so the Cycle had a single pod.
- The Sensemaking Sprint may have run about two weeks (unconfirmed). The
  guide "What to Expect: From Pod to Hackathon" describes "the two week
  sprint from pod formation to the Hackathon on August 15th."
- Summit #3 (Oct 13) closed Cycle 3 and announced the Q4 Cycle. Build
  Cycles 3 and 4 overlapped.

Events (Luma)
- Jul 25: Sensemaking Sprint ("first ever"). Pods to form; leave with a
  systems map, themes, and a field research plan.
- Mondays Aug 3, 10, 24; Sep 7, 21; Oct 12: virtual check-ins.
- Aug 11: Meet the Pods, with a "Building Out Loud" workshop. Includes a
  "paradox statement (to be used in the hackathon)."
- Aug 15: Hackathon. Pod Sprint track: "Everyone will have completed
  Assignments #1 & #2." Design Thinking workshop on the Newcomer track only.
  Page calls the Build Cycle "a thematic 12-week program."
- Aug 18 and Sep 22: GitHub workshops ("Introduction to GitHub for Team
  Projects"; "GitHub 2.0: The Handoff").
- Sep 8: Meet the Projects, teams of 3 to 7, with a storytelling workshop.
- Oct 13: Summit #3. Announces Q4 cycle: "we'll test what an 8 week build
  cycle might look like."

Dashboard timeline (as viewed Nov 5, 2026)
- Wk 0 kickoff, wk 1 problem proposals, wk 2 problem voting, wk 3 pod
  creation, wk 4 meet the pods, wk 5 experiments, wk 6 pitches, wk 7
  voting, wk 8 meet the projects, wk 9-10 mentor intensives, wk 11
  rehearsal, wk 12 showcase. No hackathon, check-ins, or assignments shown.
