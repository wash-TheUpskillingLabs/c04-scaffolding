Made-up example for the prototype. Not a real situation.

## The problem

Our team had three open pull requests and nobody knew who was allowed to merge them, so nobody did.

## What worked

We agreed in the team channel that whoever didn't write it reviews and merges it. Took five minutes once someone asked.
