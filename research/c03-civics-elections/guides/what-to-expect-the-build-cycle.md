# What to Expect: The Build Cycle

A Civics & Elections guide, as provided by a participant. Names replaced with roles. Layout from the original PDF.

```text
What the Build Cycle is:
You’re embarking on a 3 month journey where you’ll learn not just the technical skills to working
with AI, but the durable skills required to succeed in the future of work.

Because what you put in is so critical to what you get out with this technology – and because we
think the judgement of what problems matter remains uniquely human – we place a lot of
emphasis on the discovery & exploration of problem spaces, rather than jumping straight into
building solutions. You’ll use AI along the way, but you’ll also have a chance to build and
practice skills like collaboration, stakeholder engagement, user research, storytelling, and more.

   ●​ Month 1 (Problem Discovery & Definition): You’ll explore challenges that matter to
      you and to an entire industry, from real-world surveys to AI-assisted research. Through
      community voting, the most compelling problems rise to the top—and small teams called
      “pods” form around them.
   ●​ Month 2 (Exploration & Experimentation): As your pod explores your problem spaces,
      you come up with projects—proposed solutions to the challenges you’ve deeply
      understood. You’ll research and test ideas: learning AI by doing, supported by peers and
      mentors along the way.
   ●​ Month 3 (Prototype Building & Iterating): Projects turn experiments into working
      prototypes—tools, workflows, templates. Every cycle ends with a public showcase, so
      your work has visibility and impact beyond the twelve weeks. Projects can keep going at
      the end of the cycle as open-source efforts in the Labs.

What this Cycle is about:
Civics and elections covers the systems and information that make democratic participation
work. That includes election administration, voter access, civic tech and government services,
public records, media literacy, and local journalism. Pods pick specific problems in this space,
explore them deeply, and build practical solutions, all while using AI throughout the entire
process.

What to expect:
  ●​ Every month, there’s a meetup where you will present your progress with your team:
      from pods, to projects, to the showcase at the end of the cycle.
  ●​ Every week, there’s two workshops (one in-person, one virtual) meant to support where
      you are at in the cycle. You’ll also complete your learning log weekly so we know how
      best to support you.
  ●​ There are two work days: the sensemarking sprint & the hackathon. These are meant to
      help you rapidly make progress on the critical work at that phase, and to help you meet
      other Upskillers
  ●​ Most of our other work and communication happens in Slack and in the Open Labs
      Operating System (theupskillinglabs.org) – the software that powers our Build Cycle.
```
