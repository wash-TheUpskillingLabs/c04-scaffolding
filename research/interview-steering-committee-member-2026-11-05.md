# Interview: steering committee member on modules, roles and support

Filed as issue #25.

1. What it is
A steering committee member's current thinking on how modules, workshops
and support roles should work in a Build Cycle.

2. Where it came from
A steering committee member (organizer).

3. When
Nov 5, 2026.

4. What it says
- The current workshop model "doesn't work." Modules should be deployable in
  a cycle: "cards to play, but optional."
- The Cycle is "an end-to-end sprint to prove yourself with lots of
  support." Participants will run into things they don't know how to do.
- Training has no required output; workshops have outputs.
- Poderator: guides people through the process, schedules meetings, makes
  sure the cycle happens for the group. Not project management. Not fully
  fleshed out.
- Facilitator: a higher-tier role.
- A small team runs the cycle and needs these tools, plus mentors ready to
  step in.
- The Cycle shouldn't be always on. Open question: what The Labs looks like
  between cycles.
- Learning may need more on-call support: SMEs 1:1, 1:few, 1:cohort/public.
  At 1:cohort/public it "meets the threshold of a module."
- GitHub is a must.
- Module = modular content. Two kinds: upskilling modules (for learners)
  and training modules (for trainers who deliver them).
- A prototype module needs a few maintainers to create and maintain it.
