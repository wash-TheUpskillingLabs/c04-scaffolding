Made-up example for the prototype. Not a real situation.

## The problem

I fixed a participant's deployment myself because it was faster. The next week they hit the same error and messaged me again.

## What worked

The second time, I asked what the error said and what they'd tried, and waited. They found it in about ten minutes. It took longer, but they haven't asked since.
