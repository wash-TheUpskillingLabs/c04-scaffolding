```text
THE UPSKILLING LABS                                                                    Civics & Elections Cycle

Project Registration
A quick guide to choosing and joining your project team.

              Registration is open now and closes Monday, August 31 at 11:59 PM ET.

How it works
Every pitch submitted this cycle is open for registration — you choose the project you want to work on
directly. You do not need to be in a pod to join, and you can join a project from any pod. Pitches are shown
without submitter names: choose the idea, not the person.

Register in four steps
   1. Sign in at theupskillinglabs.org, open the current cycle, and select Register for a project.
   2. Read the pitches. Each project card carries a short summary. Select Read the full pitch to expand
      the four submitted answers: the refined problem statement, the project hypothesis, the target users,
      and the value.
   3. Join. Press Join on the project you want. The card confirms your registration on the spot.
   4. Changed your mind? Withdraw and join a different project — any time before the window closes.

The rules at a glance
   • One project per person. You register for a single project each cycle.
   • Five seats per project. A card marked Project full has reached its cap — pick another.
   • Three makes a team. A project becomes active once three or more members have joined; with fewer,
     it stays forming.
   • Deadline. Registration closes Monday, August 31 at 11:59 PM ET. No late joins.

After you join
When your project reaches three members it becomes active and project work begins. Watch your dashboard
for your team roster and next steps. If your first choice stays below three members, you can withdraw and
move to another project before the deadline — don’t wait for the last night to settle in.

Questions? Ask your Poderator, or write to [email removed].
```
