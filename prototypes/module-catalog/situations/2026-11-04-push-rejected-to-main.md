Made-up example for the prototype. Not a real situation.

## The problem

Two teammates had been working alone in their own copies. When they tried to put their work together, pushes to main were rejected and they thought they'd broken something.

## What worked

Drawing the branch on a whiteboard, then each of them opening a pull request instead of pushing. I asked what they expected to happen before each step rather than doing it for them.
