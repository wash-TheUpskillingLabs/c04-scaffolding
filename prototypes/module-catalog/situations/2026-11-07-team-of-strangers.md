Made-up example for the prototype. Not a real situation.

## The problem

A new team met for the first time and spent the whole hour on introductions and ideas, with no one sure who would do what by Monday.

## What worked

In the last ten minutes each person named one thing they'd do by Monday and wrote it in the team channel. It held for the week.
